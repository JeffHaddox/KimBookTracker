# Read Between

A reading journal you can install on your phone.

- **Library**: the books you have finished, with star ratings, search, genre filter and sharing.
- **To Read**: books you are considering, ranked by priority. "Finished it!" moves a book to your library.
- **Your Next Read**: suggestions based on your ratings.
- **Stats**: books and pages read for any timeframe, broken down by genre and author.
- **Add a book**: scan the barcode with your camera, snap a cover photo, or type it in. Scanned ISBNs are looked up on Google Books and Open Library.

## Install on iPhone

Open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Your data

Books are saved in the browser on the device you use. Use **Export backup** at the bottom of the page now and then, and **Import backup** to restore or move your shelf to another device.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app
- `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, icons: home-screen install and offline support
